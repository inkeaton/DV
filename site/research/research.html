Spread All Over

Over 1,200 leading institutions span six continents, from Massachusetts to Beijing.

North America and Europe dominate with over 80% of papers, but Asia's presence is growing rapidly.

University of Utah leads with 152 papers, followed by the University of California, and the University of Hong Kong. Research concentrates in tech hubs: Boston, California, Hong Kong, Beijing.

Jacks of All Trades

This diagram lists the major world institutions, and relates them to the major topics of the field.

We can see that, across the regions, interests are fairly balanced, with all of the major topics touched on by most institutions.

A faint trend that seems to emerge is that Western institutions (North America, Europe) have a slightly stronger focus on Volume Rendering and Immersive Tech, compared to Eastern ones, to seem to favor more Graph Visualization.

Making Friends
Across the Globe

261 collaborations connect the top 15 institutions. The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, leads with 131 collaborations, even though most are domestic (80%). Georgia Tech, comparatively, bridges continents with more varied collaborations.

Cross-regional partnerships are less common (16% of collaborations), most are between North America and Europe.

Key Takeaways

  1. Western regions still lead the charge, but the East is growing rapidly
  2. The biggest institutions dabble in a variety of topics
  3. Collaboration between regions is still limited to traditional partnerships
