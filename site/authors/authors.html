Unity Is Strength

The average number of authors per paper has more than doubled from 2.1 in 1990 to 5.5 in 2022.

This increase reflects the growing complexity of visualization research and the trend toward larger, more interdisciplinary collaborations.

We can notice a plateau in 2020, possibly due to the pandemic's impact on collaboration.

The shaded area shows the variance in team sizes. It shows that, while the average continues to rise, the actual team sizes follow suit, with more papers having larger author counts over time, and no solo paper being published since the late 2000s.

Add a Place at the Table

The visualization research community has grown exponentially, from 119 authors in 1990 to over 7,000 unique authors by 2024.

Recent years have seen the fastest growth, with over 350 new authors joining the community in 2020 alone. This could be an effect of the pandemic, as more researchers turned to visualization to analyze and communicate COVID-19 data.

This explosive growth reflects both the increasing importance of data visualization across fields and VIS's success in attracting diverse talent.

A Man's Career in One Bubble

This chart maps author productivity (papers) against impact (citations), with bubble size representing award count.

Huamin Qu leads in papers (70), while Jeffrey Heer's work shows exceptional citation-to-paper ratio, averaging over 250 citations per paper. Hanspeter Pfister is the most awarded author with 10 recognitions.

The great number of emerging authors (gray bubbles) shows the next generation of visualization leaders, with strong early-career productivity even without having yet accumulated the citations and awards of established researchers.

Key Takeaways

  1. Team sizes have more than doubled — from 2.2 to 5.3 authors per paper
  2. The community has grown 35x since 1990 to nearly 8,000 authors
  3. Top researchers balance high productivity (100+ papers) with exceptional impact (10,000+ citations)
  4. A new generation of emerging authors is building on this strong foundation
