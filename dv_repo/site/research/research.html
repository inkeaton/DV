A Global Community

55 leading institutions span six continents, from Massachusetts to Beijing. North America and Europe dominate with 62% of papers, but Asia's presence is growing rapidly.

University of Washington leads with 134 papers, followed by Harvard and MIT. Research concentrates in tech hubs: Boston, Bay Area, London, Beijing.

Institutional Specializations

Top institutions show distinct research profiles. University of Washington excels in InfoVis (52 papers), while UBC and University of Oxford lead in SciVis.

Stanford and MIT contribute broadly across all topics, while Georgia Tech and NYU specialize in Visual Analytics and Interaction.

Collaboration Networks

561 collaborations connect the top 15 institutions. University of Washington and Harvard lead with 45 and 42 joint papers, while MIT collaborates with 12 different institutions.

Cross-regional partnerships are common (32% of collaborations), especially between North America and Europe. UBC bridges continents with 38 international collaborations.

Key Takeaways

  1. InfoVis, SciVis, and VA form a balanced trifecta of research focus
  2. User studies are now expected, not optional
  3. Healthcare and climate are driving applied visualization research
  4. Data complexity has grown exponentially — 80 dimensions is now normal
  5. Open science has transformed from exception to norm
