What's Trending?

The visualization research landscape is constantly evolving. Machine Learning for Visualization and Immersive Analytics are seeing the highest growth rates.

Topics are color-coded by difficulty level, helping you find areas that match your current skills while showing paths for advancement.

Green topics are great starting points for newcomers!

Where to Start?

Some research areas are particularly welcoming to newcomers. User Studies, Dashboard Design, and Color Studies have lower barriers to entry.

These areas often require less technical infrastructure and can produce meaningful contributions with solid experimental design.

Click on any area to learn more about what research in that domain typically involves.

Where to Study?

Visualization research is a global endeavor. The United States leads with 42 PhD programs, followed by China and Germany.

Europe offers excellent opportunities with strong programs in the UK, France, Netherlands, Switzerland, and Austria.

Consider factors beyond rankings: research culture, advisor fit, and international collaboration opportunities matter greatly.

How Collaboration Works

Visualization papers typically have 3-4 authors. The most common pattern is a student working with their advisor and possibly a postdoc or industry collaborator.

68.5% of papers have a student as first author, making this an excellent field for student-led research.

International collaborations account for about 32% of papers, so building a global network early can be valuable.

Aiming for Excellence

IEEE VIS recognizes outstanding work through several awards. The Best Student Paper award specifically celebrates student-led contributions.

Student awards have been increasing over the years, reflecting growing investment in student research and mentorship.

Award-winning papers often feature novel techniques, rigorous evaluation, and clear presentation.

Key Takeaways for Students

  1. Start with accessible topics — User studies, dashboard design, and perception research are great entry points.
  2. Follow the trends — ML for Vis and immersive analytics are growing rapidly.
  3. Build collaborations early — The field values teamwork and international networks.
  4. Aim high — Student awards recognize excellent work, and student-first authorship is the norm.
  5. Explore globally — Great programs exist on every continent.
