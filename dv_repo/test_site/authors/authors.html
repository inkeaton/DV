Who Publishes at VIS?

PhD students are the largest contributor group, authoring over a third of all papers.

Faculty at all levels make up another 46%, while industry researchers contribute a small but growing 3% of papers.

Growing Connections

The collaboration network has grown from 85 authors in 1990 to over 2,850 today.

More importantly, the average number of collaborators per author has increased from 2.8 to 10.2 — showing a more interconnected community.

Prolific Contributors

A small group of researchers have been incredibly prolific. The top 10 authors have published over 600 papers combined.

Bubble size shows h-index — Jeffrey Heer has the highest impact-per-paper ratio, thanks to influential tools like Prefuse and Vega.

Publishing Patterns

Publication rates peak during years 11-15 of a career, when researchers are typically associate professors with active labs.

The gap between average and median shows that a few super-prolific researchers skew the distribution upward.

Toward Equity

Women's representation has increased from 12% in 1990 to 38% in 2024.

While progress has been steady, the community still has work to do to reach gender parity and address other dimensions of diversity.

Key Takeaways

  1. PhD students drive a significant portion of VIS research output
  2. The collaboration network has grown 10x more connected since 1990
  3. A small group of prolific authors have outsized influence on the field
  4. Peak productivity occurs mid-career (years 11-15)
  5. Gender diversity has tripled but more progress is needed
